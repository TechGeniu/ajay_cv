About Me

From a business graduate to, now — an official sound engineering graduate, I've come a long way, sacrificing, believing and moving places to transition to a career that I really look forward to work in. The ambition and desire stems from my love for music since a young age, genres such as boom bap, g-funk, conscious rap having a strong influence on me and music as a whole — liberating me to understand and educate myself on various topics.
